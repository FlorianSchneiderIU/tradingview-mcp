## Intra-week retest of the extreme

- Weekly LOW retested later the same week: **30%** of weeks (median 16h after the low).
- Weekly HIGH retested later the same week: **34%** of weeks (median 21h after the high).
- Retest = price leaves by 25% of the weekly range, then returns within 10% of the extreme.

## How to use

Concentrate spring-long hunting in the day/session where weekly LOWS cluster, and look for the low's retest window as a second entry. Treat clustering as a mild prior, not a guarantee - check the bars and whether the modal cell is much above the uniform 1/7 (14.3%) per day baseline.
